Easy Fix — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how the Easy Fix website handles information when you use Google sign-in and the YouTube subscription check.

Information accessed

If you choose to sign in with Google and authorize the requested access, Easy Fix uses the YouTube Data API to check whether the signed-in Google account is subscribed to the Easy Fix YouTube channel. This requires access to your YouTube subscription information.

How information is used

The subscription information is used only to determine whether the website should show the download links. The website does not need your Google password; Google handles the sign-in process.

Storage and sharing

The website may use a server-side session to remember your verification status while you use the site. Do not assume that signing out of the website revokes the permission you granted to Google. You can review or revoke the website's Google account access from your Google Account security settings.

Easy Fix does not sell personal information. Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except as needed to operate Google sign-in and the YouTube API, or when required by law.

Data retention

Session information is intended to be temporary. The website should not retain YouTube subscription data longer than needed to perform the subscription check. If the website's technical configuration changes, this policy should be updated to describe the actual practices.

Children

If you are not old enough to consent to online services in your location, use the website only with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or data practices change. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the Easy Fix team through the Easy Fix YouTube channel.
